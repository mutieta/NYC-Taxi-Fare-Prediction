# Introductions

Ride-hailing platforms in Cambodia such as WowNow, Grab, PassApp, and TADA are growing rapidly.

However, current ride pricing mainly depends on distance and travel time, without fully considering factors such as demand surges, weather conditions, holidays, airport charges, and location-based patterns.

This can lead to inefficient pricing strategies. Therefore, machine learning approaches using spatiotemporal features and historical demand data may help improve fare prediction accuracy and transportation management.

# Problem

* **Limitations of Traditional Methods:** Simple pricing models based only on distance and travel duration cannot capture complex fare patterns.
* **Static Pricing:** Current ride fares rely on fixed calculations that may not reflect real-time demand and environmental conditions.
* **Loss of Revenue:** Inaccurate pricing during peak hours can reduce ride availability and affect company and driver revenue.

# Goals

## Business Goals

* Maximize revenues profits.
* Create a dynamic pricing model.

## Analytical Goals

* Compare the accuracy and efficiency of Random Forest, Ridge Regression, XGBoost, and LightGBM models.
* Identify the most important features contributing to fare prediction through feature importance analysis.
